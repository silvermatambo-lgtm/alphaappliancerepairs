Ask on WhatsApp ↗
Which appliances can you assist with?+

We receive repair enquiries for fridges, freezers, televisions, washing machines, dishwashers, tumble dryers, ovens, stoves, microwaves, gas stoves, ice machines, coffee machines and air conditioning.

How do I request a repair?+

Call or WhatsApp +27 67 012 0750, or use the contact form to create a WhatsApp enquiry. Describe the appliance, brand, symptoms and your location.

Do I need to know the exact fault?+

No. Simply describe what the appliance is doing or not doing, and mention any error code or unusual sound.

Can I send a photo of the appliance?+

Yes. Start a WhatsApp conversation and share clear photos or the model number to help explain the issue.

Do you repair gas stoves?+

Gas stove enquiries are accepted. Any gas-related work must be assessed and completed by an appropriately registered or certified professional.

Do you have a call-out charge or warranty?+

Pricing, call-out charges, availability and any applicable repair warranty should be confirmed directly with Alpha Appliance before work is booked.

What areas do you service?+

Please share your suburb, city and province when you enquire so the team can confirm availability for your location.
